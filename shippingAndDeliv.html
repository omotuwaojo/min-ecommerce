What items can I send with Minstores Delivery?

+

You can ship a wide range of items, including documents, personal belongings, electronics, and business merchandise. However, for safety and legal compliance, certain items are prohibited, such as illegal substances, flammable materials, and live animals. For a full list of restricted items, please refer to our guidelines.

How long does delivery take with Minstores Delivery?

+

Delivery times depend on your selected service and destination. For standard nationwide deliveries in Nigeria, expect 1–5 working days, depending on distance. You’ll receive an estimated delivery timeframe when you drop off your package.

How should I package my items?

+

Pack with care to protect your shipment. Use a sturdy box, cushion fragile items with bubble wrap, and seal your package securely to prevent damage in transit.

What if my parcel is delayed or lost?

+

We aim to deliver every package on time, but occasional delays may occur due to unforeseen circumstances. You can track your shipment in real-time using our online tool. If your parcel is lost though rare please contact our customer support team right away for assistance.

How can I contact Minstores Delivery customer support?

+

Our dedicated customer support team is ready to assist you! You can contact us by phone at 0201 888 1106, via email, or through our live chat during business hours.

What are your operational hours for pickup and drop-off?

+

Pickup and drop-off station hours differ by location but typically run from 9 AM to 5 PM, Monday through Friday, and 10 AM to 2 PM on Saturdays. Check specific hours for your nearest station.

RETURN POLICY

At our backpack and bag online store, we want to make sure you are completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not satisfied with your item, we offer a 7-day return and exchange policy. Please read the following terms and conditions carefully before proceeding with your return.

Return and Exchange Conditions:

  1. You may return or exchange any item within 7 days of receiving it. The 7-day period begins on the date the item is delivered to you.
  2. Items must be returned in their original condition, with all tags and labels intact, and in their original packaging.
  3. Items must not have been used or washed.
  4. All returns and exchanges are subject to approval by our customer service team.

Return and Exchange Process:

  1. To initiate a return or exchange, please contact our customer service team within 7 days of receiving your item. You can reach us by email or phone.
  2. Our customer service team will provide you with a return address and instructions on how to send the item back to us.
  3. Once we receive your returned item, we will inspect it to ensure it meets our return and exchange conditions.
  4. If the item meets our conditions, we will issue a store credit for the full purchase price, including any taxes or shipping charges. The store credit will be sent to you via email and can be used towards future purchases on our website.
  5. If you wish to exchange the item for a different product, we will issue a store credit for the full purchase price of the returned item, and you can use that credit towards your new purchase.
  6. If the item does not meet our return and exchange conditions, we reserve the right to refuse the return or exchange.

Note:

  1. We do not offer refunds to the original payment method.
  2. The customer is responsible for all shipping and handling charges related to returns and exchanges.
  3. We are not responsible for any items lost or damaged during the return shipping process. Please ensure that you use a reliable shipping method and obtain a tracking number.

If you have any questions or concerns about our return policy, please do not hesitate to contact our customer service team. We are always here to help you.
